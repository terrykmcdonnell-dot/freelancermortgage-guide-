Last updated: April 28, 2025

FreelancerMortgage.guide ("this site," "we," "us") is committed to being transparent about how it operates. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Who we are

FreelancerMortgage.guide is an independent informational website providing resources for self-employed individuals navigating the mortgage process. We have no affiliation with any mortgage lender, broker, or financial institution.

Data we collect

The readiness assessment: The assessment tool runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are never transmitted to our servers, stored in a database, or associated with any identifying information. When you close the tab, the results are gone. We designed it this way deliberately.

Usage analytics: Like most websites, we use analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are visited, how long visitors stay, and where they come from. This data is aggregated and anonymous. We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information through analytics.

Advertising: This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads that are relevant to your interests, based on your browsing history across websites. This is standard behaviour for AdSense placements.

Cookies

This site uses cookies in the following ways:

  • Analytics cookies — set by our analytics provider to measure site usage. These are anonymous and aggregate.
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google AdSense to serve relevant advertisements. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
  • No functional cookies — we do not set any cookies required for site functionality. The readiness assessment does not use cookies.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of analytics tracking by using your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker.

Third-party services

This site uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:
