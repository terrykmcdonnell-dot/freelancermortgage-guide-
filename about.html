About this site

What this is and why it exists

FreelancerMortgage.guide is an independent resource for self-employed workers, freelancers, and contractors navigating the mortgage process. It has no affiliation with any lender, broker, or financial institution.

The problem we're trying to solve

Search for "freelancer mortgage" and you'll find pages of results — helpful-sounding guides, explainers, calculators, checklists. Almost all of them are written by mortgage lenders or brokers. Almost all of them end with a contact form, a rate quote button, or a call to action to speak with their loan officers.

There is nothing wrong with that. Lenders need to market their services. But it means the information available to freelancers trying to understand their situation is filtered through a commercial lens. The goal of the person writing is to get you to apply — not necessarily to tell you whether you should.

This site exists to fill that gap. An honest, independent assessment of what lenders actually look for, what makes self-employed applications harder, and what you can genuinely do to improve your position.

What we believe

Honesty over helpfulness theatre
It's more useful to tell someone they're not ready yet — and explain specifically why — than to give them generic advice that makes them feel informed without actually helping them.
No hidden agenda
This site displays advertising. Advertisers do not influence the content. A mortgage lender advertising here does not constitute an endorsement. The articles say what they say regardless of who's buying ads.
No data collection
The readiness assessment runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are never stored, transmitted, or associated with any identifying information. When you close the tab, they're gone.
Independent of the market it covers
We have no referral relationships with brokers, no affiliate arrangements with lenders, and no financial stake in whether you apply for a mortgage or not.

A note on the content

The articles on this site are written to be as accurate and useful as possible, but they are not financial or legal advice. Mortgage lending rules vary by country, lender, and individual circumstance. Always verify information with a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

The readiness assessment produces a verdict based on the factors you provide. It cannot account for every lender's specific criteria, and its verdict should be treated as a starting point for thinking, not a guarantee of any outcome.
